HIL

Interventions go in the replay buffer and are trained on with the plain task reward — no optimism bonus. The agent values only what reaches the goal, so it learns slowly and rarely exploits your help.

Q-OIL no decoupling

A single critic gets the +b bonus on intervention transitions. Because it bootstraps from itself, the bonus leaks backward into the failing pre-intervention states — watch max Q blow up (over-optimism).

Q-OIL decoupled

Two critics. The optimistic one gets +b but bootstraps from the task critic, so the bonus stays confined to intervention states: optimistic guidance, not optimal supervision. It biases exploration toward your guidance, then fades back to Q*.
